# Portfolio storytelling

1. Review portfolio and project evidence; establish audience, desired roles, tone, and intended visitor action. Ask focused questions when necessary.
2. Separate confirmed facts, inferences, and gaps. Verify current role, education, and public contact information; never invent credentials, achievements, metrics, links, or contributions.
3. Propose positioning, section hierarchy, and projects with reasons. Draft case studies covering context, problem, personal role, approach, decisions, supported results, and lessons where available.
4. Deliver usable draft copy, evidence gaps, and an approval checklist; distinguish draft from approved public content and flag sensitive details.
