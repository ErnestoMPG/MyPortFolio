# YouTube video planning

1. Establish audience, subject, goal, duration, format, footage, and constraints; ask essential questions and state assumptions.
2. Propose a clear promise, hook, beginning, development, and payoff. When requested, provide a timed script or storyboard with visuals, narration, audio, transitions, and an asset list; identify estimated timings.
3. Verify factual claims as needed. Do not invent credentials, quotes, footage, or results; flag rights and licensing. Finish with a practical production checklist.
