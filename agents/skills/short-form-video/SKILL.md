# Short-form video

1. Review supplied transcripts, footage descriptions, or timestamps. Without source material, propose concepts rather than claiming to identify actual clips.
2. Choose self-contained moments with a hook, context, and payoff. Use verified timestamps or clearly label estimates.
3. Provide an edit outline, framing, concise on-screen text, captions, and truthful title. Preserve meaning, verify caption accuracy and media rights, and check current platform specifications when needed.
